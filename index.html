Our core service

Presence-less online registration

The Tamil Nadu Registration Department now lets certain documents be registered online, with parties authenticated through Aadhaar instead of appearing before the Sub-Registrar. We take care of every step so the document is registered correctly the first time.

For banks & borrowers 01

Bank MOD

Memorandum of Deposit of Title Deeds

When a loan is secured by property, the mortgage by deposit of title deeds is registered online, so it reflects in the Encumbrance Certificate and protects the lender.

  • Scrutiny of title deeds and loan sanction terms
  • Drafting of the MOD as per the bank's format
  • Stamp duty and registration fee calculation
  • Aadhaar authentication of borrower and bank officer
  • Registered MOD delivered to the bank

Discharge

How it works

From documents to a registered deed in six steps

You don't queue at the Sub-Registrar Office. We prepare everything and guide each party through Aadhaar authentication.

Share your documents

Send copies of the title deeds, patta, EC, IDs and, for bank work, the sanction letter or closure letter.

Title scrutiny

Our team reviews the documents and flags any gap in title or approvals before anything is filed.

Drafting & fee calculation

We draft the document and calculate stamp duty, registration fee and other charges as per the Department's rules.

Aadhaar authentication

Each executing party and witness is authenticated with a UIDAI-compliant fingerprint or iris device, by arrangement.

Online payment & submission

Fees are paid online and the document is submitted to the jurisdictional Sub-Registrar through TNREGINET.

Registered document

Once the Sub-Registrar registers it, the digitally signed document is delivered to you or the bank.

Aadhaar authentication is required for every executing party. Details in the document cannot be changed once parties are authenticated, which is why we complete the scrutiny and drafting carefully first. If authentication fails, the document may need to be registered in the regular way at the SRO; we will guide you on that.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is presence-less registration?

It is online registration through the Tamil Nadu Registration Department's TNREGINET portal, where the parties are identified through Aadhaar biometric authentication instead of appearing in person before the Sub-Registrar.

Which documents can be registered this way?

Our presence-less service covers Bank MODs (deposit of title deeds), discharges (deed of receipt / release), and first sale of plots and apartments. The Department may add or change eligible documents; we will tell you whether your document qualifies.

Do I still have to visit the Sub-Registrar Office?

No, as long as Aadhaar authentication of all parties succeeds. Each party authenticates with a fingerprint or iris device, which we arrange.

Can resale properties be registered presence-less?

At present the presence-less option for sale deeds applies to first sales by the promoter or builder. Resale transactions are registered in the regular way, and we can help with those too.

How are the fees worked out?

Stamp duty and registration fee depend on the type of document, the loan amount or property value, and the Department's rules. We give you the government charges and our professional fee separately before we start.

How long does it take?

Once the documents are complete and all parties are authenticated, submission is quick. The final timing depends on the Sub-Registrar's processing.
